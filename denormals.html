Denormalized Float Checker

The purpose of this utility is to check for denormalized floats (also known as subnormal numbers). Paste 32-bit floats down below to check them.

Some examples are:

  • 0x3F800000 (Normal)
  • 0x7F800000 (Normal)
  • 0xFF800000 (Normal)
  • 0x7FC00000 (Normal)
  • 0x00000001 (Denormal)
  • 0x80000002 (Denormal)
  • 0x007FFFFF (Denormal)
